Waypoint

A personal travel companion for US & Canada road trips. Plan with an AI wizard, track destinations, restaurants, lodging, and special-interest places, and manage expenses, fuel, packing lists, and journal entries — all in one installable, mobile-friendly PWA.

View on GitHub Explore Features

What Waypoint Does

A full travel-planning companion — from first idea to the drive home.

🤖

AI Trip Wizard

Describe a trip and Claude plans the route while Google Places enriches every stop with lodging, restaurants, and points of interest — all reviewable before you save.

🗺️

Trip Companion View

A two-panel layout with a stop-by-stop timeline alongside a live Google Maps route, plus tabs for expenses, packing lists, journal entries, and fuel logs. Pin a stop to your exact hotel, rental, or campsite and get turn-by-turn directions to it or to your next stop.

📍

Destinations & Places

Track destinations, restaurants, lodging, and special-interest spots like wineries, museums, and parks — each with photo galleries, ratings, maps, and AI suggestions.

⚡

Quick Add Sheets

Places-search-powered bottom sheets let you add any entity type in seconds, from the global "+" button or any list page — built for mobile-first use.

📊

Stats Dashboard

Charts for destinations, trips, spending, and road stats — including total miles, fuel cost, and cost-per-mile — powered by ng2-charts.

📱

Installable PWA

Offline-capable app shell with service-worker caching and an install-prompt banner — add it to your home screen and use it like a native app.

Built With

A modern, fully typed stack across the frontend, backend, and infrastructure.

Frontend

Angular 17 — standalone components, signals, lazy-loaded routes, OnPush change detection

UI

Angular Material 17 — teal & amber theme, responsive sidebar / bottom-tab shell

Backend

Node.js 20, Express 4, TypeScript — hot reload with tsx watch

Database

PostgreSQL 16 with an auto-migration runner

AI

Anthropic Claude (claude-sonnet-4-5) for trip planning, suggestions, and summaries

Maps & Weather

Google Maps JS API for places & routing, Open-Meteo for forecasts

Storage

Cloudflare R2 (S3-compatible) for photo uploads

Hosting

Docker, nginx, and Railway with Cloudflare DNS
